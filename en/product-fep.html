FEP Film

FEPFEP Film

FEP film is a hot melt extruded casting film made of FEP resin (fluorinated ethylene propylene copolymer). FEP film offers almost all of the excellent properties of fluorine film, such as high temperature resistance, 化工 corrosion resistance, non-stick properties and excellent electrical properties. FEP film can be thermowelded, thermoformed, and can be combined with a variety of materials, and is widely used in biomedicine, electronic manufacturing, insulation and anti-corrosion fields.

PROPERTIES / MATERIAL CHARACTERISTICS

Material characteristics

01

Non-stick properties

Excellent non-stick and low surface friction properties make the material surface less likely to adhere to other substances.

02

Light transmittance

High all-over light transmittance of up to 92% allows light to pass through the material virtually unobstructed.

03

Corrosion resistance

Excellent 化工 stability, resistant and corrosion-resistant to a wide range of 化工s.

04

Mechanical properties

It has been tested many times for mechanical properties and maintains its original shape under high pressure and strong external forces.

05

Heat resistance

Long-term stable working temperature up to 205°C, maintain stability under high temperature conditions, not easy to deformation.

06

Electrical performance

Low conductivity, high radiation resistance, with the advantage of cross-linking through high-energy radiation, effectively isolating currents and electric fields.
