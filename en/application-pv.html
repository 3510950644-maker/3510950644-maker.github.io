Photovoltaics

Photovoltaic 应用行业页

Engineered for the next generation of solar energy, our ETFE films deliver exceptional thermal stability, superior non-stick properties, and outstanding mechanical flexibility.

These advanced fluoropolymer membranes offer robust protection against extreme weather, UV radiation, and 化工 corrosion — making them the ideal choice for flexible solar cell encapsulation. With PLUSXTECH ETFE, you achieve longer module lifespan, reduced maintenance, and consistently higher energy output even in the harshest photovoltaic environments.

Photovoltaic panel application

In the realm of photovoltaic panels, VitaFlon® film provides reliable protection for solar panels against harsh environments and 化工s, thanks to its exceptional 化工 resistance, high-temperature resistance, and mechanical properties. It is particularly well-suited for flexible solar cell packaging, making it an ideal choice for the photovoltaic industry, contributing to more efficient and durable solar energy solutions.

02

Solar cell energy storage system

Sunlight is a sustainable and renewable source of clean energy. In arid desert regions, integrated solar heat collection systems for water and electricity production are critical for achieving sustainable development. VitaFlon® film offers excellent 化工 resistance, high-temperature resistance, and recyclability. It significantly improves solar panel energy conversion efficiency, extends lifespan, and reduces long-term maintenance costs, making it the optimal choice for solar energy utilization in desert environments.
