PTFE Film

PTFE, or Polytetrafluoroethylene, is a synthetic polymer Polytetrafluoroethylene. It has extremely low surface energy, so it has excellent 化工 resistance, heat resistance, cold resistance, electrical insulation and non-adhesion. Known as the “King of plastics”, PTFE are widely used in national economic fields such as 化工s, pharmaceuticals, food, machinery, 电子, 电子, military industry, 航空, environmental protection and bridges.

Corrosion ResistantNon-stick PropertyElectric InsulationLow Friction PropertyHigh Temperature ResistantInsulation
PFAPFA Film

PFA Film

PLUSXTECH PFA (Polyfluoroalkoxy)film is a copolymer of perfluoropropyl perfluorovinyl ether and polytetrafluoroethylene, which has great 化工-resistance and high-temperature stability to maintain excellent performance at extreme temperatures, thus greatly improving productivity.Now it is widely used in the manufacture of 化工, electronic and appliances, automobiles, 医药 equipment, video processing equipment, metallurgical equipment and other product.

Chemical ResistanceElectrical InsulationWear-resistant And Non-crackingAnti-adhesionHeat-resistantHigh Transparency
FEPFEP Film

FEP Film

PLUSXTECH FEP film is a highly functional composite film made of tetrafluoroethylene and hexafluoropropylene resin by a melt extrusion casting process.It combines many advantages of fluoropolymer film, including excellent high-temperature and 化工 resistance, and can be used in the temperature range of -240℃～205℃ for a long time.It is also a kind of excellent high-functioning flame-retardant product.

Strong Signal TransmissionHigh InsulationChemical ResistanceOxidation ResistanceHigh And Low Temperature ResistanceFlame Retardancy
ETFEETFE Film

ETFE Film

PLUSXTECH ETFE film is a kind of fluoroplastic film made from ethylene-tetrafluoroethylene copolymer resin by melt extrusion casting process. It maintains the original excellent performance of fluorine film on the basis of comprehensively optimizing and upgrading the performance to create an ideal aviation, solar energy and composite material release material.

Self-cleaningCorrosion ResistantHeat WeldableHigh Tear ResistanceHigh Temperature ResistanceHigh Transparency

PVDF Film

PVDF film is made from PVDF modified master batch.It has diverse characteristics such as excellent weather resistance, high temperature resistance, organic solvent resistance, acid&alkali resistance, and strong self-cleaning ability.PVDF film is widely used in the field of outdoor as photovoltaic protective film, outdoor 建材 material protective film, outdoor capacitor film, and film layer for electric heating type,etc.
