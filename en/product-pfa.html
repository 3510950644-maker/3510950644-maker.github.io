PFA Film

PFAPFA Film

PFA film is produced from PFA resin(copolymer of perfluoropropyl perfluoroethylene ether and polytetrafluoroethylene) by a melt extrusion casting process, which has excellent 化工 resistance and high-temperature stability, and maintains its excellent performance at extreme temperatures, thus greatly improving productivity. Now it is widely used in the manufacture of 化工, electronic and electrical appliances, automobiles, 医药 equipment, video processing equipment, metallurgical equipment and other 产品页.

PROPERTIES / MATERIAL CHARACTERISTICS

Material characteristics

01

Non-stick

The unique molecular structure provides extremely low surface energy and reduces adhesion between substances, making it a popular choice for industrial components.

02

High-quality electrical properties

extremely low power factor and dielectric constant, not easy to occur electrical breakdown, to protect the safety and reliable operation of electronic equipment and circuits.

03

Weather Resistant and Durable

It is not easily affected by natural factors such as UV rays and extreme temperatures in outdoor and harsh climatic conditions, and reduces degradation or damage due to climatic and environmental factors.

04

Thermal stability

It can withstand high temperature to 300 ° C for a short time, generally can be used continuously between 240 ° C and 260 ° C, and does not melt at high temperature.

05

Low permeability

PFA films have extremely low permeability to gases, liquids, moisture and organic vapors and excel in demanding encapsulation, sealing and liquid transfer 应用行业页.

06

Corrosion resistance

PFA films are 化工ly inert and solvent resistant to virtually all 化工s, making them popular in fields such as 化工s, petro化工s, laboratories and 医药 equipment.
