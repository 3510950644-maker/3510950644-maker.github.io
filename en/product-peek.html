PEEK Film

PEEKPEEK Film

PEEK film is a refined film product produced through the extrusion and casting process of semi-crystalline resin (polyaryletherketone composed of vinyl, ether, and ketone groups). PEEK film offers various performance characteristics, providing high-quality high-temperature resistance, water decomposition resistance, excellent sliding properties, and outstanding radiation resistance suitable for continuous service temperatures up to 300°C. PEEK film exhibits excellent dielectric properties over a wide range of temperatures and frequencies, high resistance to 化工 corrosion, and mechanical strength, making it an ideal material for industries such as 航空, automotive, 医药 devices, 电子, and 化工 engineering.

PROPERTIES / MATERIAL CHARACTERISTICS

Material characteristics

01

Mechanical Properties

PEEK film exhibits exceptional mechanical performance, including high strength, modulus, and rigidity. It also shows good fatigue resistance and impact resistance.

02

High-Temperature Stability

PEEK film maintains good thermal stability in high-temperature environments and can withstand working temperatures above 250°C.

03

Chemical Stability

It exhibits high resistance to various 化工s, including acids, alkalis, and organic solvents.

04

Insulation

PEEK film possesses low dielectric constant and low dielectric loss, making it widely used in electrical and electronic 应用行业页.

05

Biocompatibility

It shows good compatibility with biological tissues, finding widespread 应用行业页 in the 医药 device field, particularly in orthopedic implants.

06

Processability

PEEK film has good processability and can be molded into the desired parts through injection molding, extrusion, hot pressing, and other methods.
