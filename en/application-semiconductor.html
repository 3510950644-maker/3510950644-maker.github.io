Semiconductor 应用行业页

VitaFlon® film is a premium fluoroplastic engineered for the most demanding 半导体 environments.

Delivering exceptional dielectric strength and thermal stability, it serves as a reliable high-temperature barrier and superior electrical insulation for wafer grids and IC chip encapsulation. With near-zero outgassing and outstanding 化工 inertness, VitaFlon® helps maximize production yield and ensures long-term reliability in next-generation 半导体 manufacturing.

Wafer and chip packaging application

In the realm of wafer and chip packaging, VitaFlon® film, as a high-performance engineering plastic, provides exceptional electrical insulation and high-temperature resistance for wafer and chip packaging. It ensures stable and reliable chip operation in harsh working environments, making it an ideal choice for high-performance electronic device packaging.

Semiconductor equipment protection application

In the realm of 半导体 equipment protection, VitaFlon® film, as a high-performance engineering plastic, provides comprehensive protection for 半导体 equipment in harsh environments, thanks to its exceptional thermal conductivity, electrical insulation, weather resistance, and 化工 resistance.
