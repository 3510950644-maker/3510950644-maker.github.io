PVDF Film

PVDFPVDF Film

PVDF film is made from PVDF modified master batch.It has diverse characteristics such as excellent weather resistance, high temperature resistance, organic solvent resistance, acid&alkali resistance, and strong self-cleaning ability.PVDF film is widely used in the field of outdoor as photovoltaic protective film, outdoor 建材 material protective film, outdoor capacitor film, and film layer for electric heating type,etc.

PROPERTIES / MATERIAL CHARACTERISTICS

Material characteristics

01

Weather resistance

It can meet the use of various extreme climates and environments, delay the degree of aging, and provide long-time outdoor protection.

02

Electrical insulation property

It has stable electrical insulation,low dielectric constant and dielectric loss,and can effectively prevent current leakage.

03

Corrosion resistance

PVDF film has excellent corrosion resisrance to a variety of common acids,bases and other 化工 reagents and is wildly applied in all kinds of corrosion resistance requirements.

04

Self-cleaning preformance

It has self-cleaning preformance ande its surface energy is low,not easily contaminated with dust and dirt,easy to clean.

05

Aging resistance

It has excellent stability of resistance to ultraviolet rays and oxidants such as oxygen,and can keep its appearance and properties in long-term exposure to sunlight and air.

06

Mechanical properties

It has high mechanical strength able to withstand bending,stretching,compression and other stresses,not easy to brittle fracture.
