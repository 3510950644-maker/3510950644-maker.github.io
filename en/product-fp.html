FP Composite Film

FPFP Composite Film

FEP-FP is a composite material made of FEP fluoroplastic (fluorinated ethylene propylene copolymer) resin cast film and PET resin (polyethylene terephthalate). FEP film combines many advantages of fluoropolymer films, such as excellent heat resistance, 化工 resistance, non-stick properties and dielectric performance.

PROPERTIES / MATERIAL CHARACTERISTICS

Material characteristics

01

Pure base material formula

Contains no plasticizers, additives or other extra materials; the material is pure.

02

Strong 化工 resistance

The material has excellent 化工 inertness and solvent resistance, and can withstand most 化工s.

03

Low-friction non-stick surface

Excellent non-stick properties, low surface friction coefficient, good release and anti-sticking performance.

04

Wide temperature range

Stable for long-term use from -70℃ to 180℃, with short-term resistance up to 230℃, and stable performance under high and low temperature conditions.
