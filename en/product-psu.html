PSU Film

PSUPSU Film

PSU film is a high-performance film made from PSU polysulfone resin through a high-temperature extrusion casting process. It combines many advantages of polysulfone-based high-performance thermoplastic films, such as excellent heat resistance, outstanding mechanical strength, good 化工 resistance, prominent dielectric properties, high transparency and dimensional stability. PSU film can be thermoformed, compounded with a variety of substrates and used as a high-performance laminating adhesive, and can be adapted for sterilization through downstream processing, meeting demanding requirements across multiple fields.
