PSU Film

PSU film is a high-performance film made from PSU polysulfone resin through a high-temperature extrusion casting process. PSU film can be thermoformed, compounded with a variety of substrates and used as a high-performance laminating adhesive, and can be adapted for sterilization through downstream processing, meeting demanding requirements across multiple fields.
