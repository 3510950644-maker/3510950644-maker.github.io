PTFE Film

PTFEPTFE Film

PTFE, also known as polytetrafluoroethylene, is a synthetic high-molecular-weight material. It has an extremely low surface energy, 化工, high and low temperature resistance, electrical insulation and non-stick properties.As the "King of Plastics" ,PTFE has widespread 应用行业页 in various fields of the national economy, including 化工, pharmaceutical, food, mechanical, electronic, electrical, military, 航空, environmental protection, and bridge construction.

PROPERTIES / MATERIAL CHARACTERISTICS

Material characteristics

01

Chemical Resistance

PTFE is inert to almost all 化工s and resists attack by strong acids, bases, organic solvents and oxidants over a wide temperature range (-250°C to +260°C).

02

Non-stick Property

Due to its low surface energy, PTFE surface is very smooth and not easily adhered to by other substances, which makes it widely used in non-stick pans, pipe linings, seals and other fields.

03

Heat Resistance

PTFE can maintain its physical and 化工 properties at high temperatures, has a very high melting point (about 327°C) and excellent the heat resistance.

04

Electrical Insulating Property

PTFE is an excellent insulating material with extremely high resistivity and extremely low dielectric constant, suitable for electrical and electronic 应用行业页 domain.

05

Low Friction

PTFE has an extremely low coefficient of friction, the smallest of all plastics, which makes it a wide range of 应用行业页 in lubrication and friction reduction.

06

Insulation Property

PTFE is an excellent insulating material and can be used for wire and cable The insulation layer.
