01

Architecture application

FlexFlon® films empower architects to create iconic, organic structures without compromising on safety or durability. Combining high-tensile strength with a rich variety of colors and high-transmittance finishes, our membranes are built to withstand the most hostile climates—from extreme sub-zero exposure (-200°C) to intense heat. With an extraordinary 300% elongation capacity, FlexFlon™ adapts to complex geometric forms with ease. Furthermore, our films provide a superior safety profile: they are fire-resistant and engineered to prevent the formation of molten droplets during thermal stress, making them the premier choice for sustainable, high-safety 建材 envelopes.

02

Application of exterior walls

PLUSXTECH ETFE films feature a non-stick, low-friction surface with exceptionally low surface energy, effectively preventing the adhesion of dust, pollutants, and debris. This inherent self-cleaning capability allows rainwater to naturally wash away surface contaminants, maintaining pristine optical clarity for years. Furthermore, the material’s smooth texture ensures that snow and ice shed effortlessly from skylights and facades, maximizing natural daylighting even in harsh winter conditions. By choosing ETFE, 建材 owners benefit from a low-maintenance 建材 envelope that significantly reduces long-term cleaning costs and operational overhead.
