ETFE Film

ETFEETFE Film

ETFE film is a kind of fluoroplastic film made of ethylene-tetrafluoroethylene copolymer as the main material, which is cast and processed by melt extrusion process. PLUSXTECH ETFE film maintains the original excellent performance of fluorine element film on the basis of a full range of optimization and upgrading of the performance of ETFE film, to create an ideal aviation, solar energy and composite material release material.

PROPERTIES / MATERIAL CHARACTERISTICS

Material characteristics

01

Mechanical properties

Maintain the original shape and size when subjected to external forces, withstand high pressure tear resistance and reach 250,000 fold fatigue tests.

02

High-temp resistance

There is no obvious morphological change under high temperature conditions, and the instantaneous working temperature can reach 260℃.

03

Corrosion resistance

Excellent 化工 inertness and solvent resistance, resistantto corrosion of most 化工 substances.

04

Electrical performance

Good isolation of current and electric field, excellent electrical performance over a wide range of wave frequencies and temperatures. Light transmittance: Excellent UV transmittance of up to 92% allows light to pass through the material without hindrance.

05

Light transmittance

Excellent UV transmittance of up to 92% allows light to pass through the material without hindrance.

06

Weather resistance

Maintaining its original performance and appearance in various harsh environments, it can withstand working temperatures from -200 ° C to 165 ° C.
