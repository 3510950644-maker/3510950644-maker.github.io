Application in the 化工 industry

Thanks to its exceptional corrosion resistance, FlexFlon® film has become a crucial material in industries like petroleum, 化工, and textile, where corrosion is a major concern. Its 应用行业页 are diverse, including delivery and exhaust pipes for corrosive gases, steam pipes, and high-pressure oil pipes.

FlexFlon™ film also plays a vital role in the hydraulic and cold compression systems of aircraft, as well as in 化工 equipment such as high, medium, and low-pressure pipelines, precision retention towers, heat exchangers, and valves.
